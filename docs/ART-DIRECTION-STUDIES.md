# Obsidian Ridge Labs: art direction studies

**Archived:** superseded by the user's Raycast, Family, Chronicle, Linear, and Nothing references. These study files remain as source history but are no longer production build entries. The implemented direction is documented in `LUMINOUS-DIRECTION.md`.

These are three working React studies, not a claim that the site is finished or award-worthy. The current public-page implementation was rejected. Its clipping defects have been addressed separately so the choice of a new direction is not mixed with a basic rendering repair.

Open `/design-directions.html` in the local preview. The review controls switch between three complete openings, each followed by an argument, an interaction, and product evidence. The page is marked noindex and is absent from the public sitemap. It has its own CSS and entry point, without the rejected site's animation or style cascade.

## What failed

The prior design repeated a large headline next to an abstract instrument. That made three different apps look interchangeable. Coordinates, figure numbers, and a chrome sculpture added atmosphere without developing the premise. The copy repeated the doctrine's short cadence but often omitted the concrete observation beneath it. The actual software arrived too late.

The clipping had separate, identifiable causes: SplitText word masks around very tight line boxes, display-size floors that exceeded narrow columns, and pinned sections that assumed their contents would fit. The new fixes remove the masks, restore native wrapping after reveals, allow natural content height, and measure chapter content before enabling the horizontal pin.

The three studies preserve the home H1, actual release status, the Apple focus, and the documented functions of the apps. Memora remains a flashcard/study app. The first two sections of the Philosophy page have not been rewritten.

## Reference lessons

- [Electronic Materials Office](https://electronicmaterialsoffice.com/) uses real product views, mechanisms, measurable comparisons, software, and recording conditions to make a product understandable. The lesson is to make imagery carry evidence. Its [official award record](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/electronic-materials-office) is an Honorable Mention.
- [Superlist](https://www.superlist.com/) establishes the product through recognizable work and interface imagery. Product clarity does not require a generic SaaS layout. Its [studio award record](https://www.awwwards.com/unseenstudio/?library=true&previewmode=true) records an older version; the current site should not be treated as the awarded build.
- [Opal](https://op.al/) currently presents a founder letter around a concrete table of prototypes. The relevant lesson is an identifiable subject and speaker. The current page differs from the site in its historical award record.
- [Unseen Studio](https://unseen.co/) gives interaction one coherent experiential role. The lesson is commitment to one idea, not an unrelated effect for each section. Its [official studio record](https://www.awwwards.com/unseenstudio/?library=true&previewmode=true) documents recognition.

These were primary-source content and award-record reads. Current animations were not inspected frame by frame. Awwwards weights design, usability, creativity, and content; WebGL is not a prerequisite. See an [official scoring page](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/my-little-storybook) and the [mobile guidelines](https://www.awwwards.com/mobile-excellence-guidelines.pdf).

## Before calling a direction finished

Choose the narrative and composition, carry it through a real product detail page, then inspect the actual running site on desktop and mobile. Check narrow widths, text enlargement, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, long headings, and route transitions. Measure loading and interaction behavior. A successful build does not establish visual quality.
