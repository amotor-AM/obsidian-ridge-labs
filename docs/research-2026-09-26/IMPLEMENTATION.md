# Approved website implementation

Completed September 26, 2026. The founder authorized implementation after the research proposal and required the Brand Doctrine update first. That sequence was followed.

## What changed

- Brand Doctrine: incorporated research limits, per-product narrative briefs, source/release claim standards, direct released-app actions beside the boundary, full-name rules, and removal of the universal closing slogan.
- Homepage: concrete category introduction; functioning collection CTA; transcript-first Echo Chamber viewer without the decorative waveform; full-width Mettle and Memora sections with equivalent finished artwork and top-aligned copy; one studio argument; qualified offline example; useful collection close.
- Echo Chamber: recording-to-retrieval-to-use story, real screen controls, direct App Store actions, platform requirements, and a separate network/storage explanation. No unsupported WER, speaker, or precise offer claims.
- Nine development apps: distinct headlines and workflows, actual preview selectors, accurate entitlement and compatibility details, portability, and app-specific boundaries. Shared tokens provide continuity; the homepage manifesto is not repeated.
- Supporting content: full names in wordmarks, accessibility text, metadata, feeds, and generated summaries; accurate collection status; corrected audited facts in help, comparisons, FAQs, and technical privacy explanations.
- Motion: existing Lenis/GSAP architecture retained; restrained artwork depth extends to the revised sections and Echo Chamber. Copy remains in normal flow.

The Philosophy hero, Silence the Cloud background, and threat-model passage were preserved. Later headings lost unnecessary forced line breaks.
