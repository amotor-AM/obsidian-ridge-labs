## Method

1. Compared current home/product copy, shared layout, artwork, CSS, product data, FAQs, and related claims.
2. Inspected the live local preview in the connected in-app browser. Read-only DOM geometry checks covered desktop and mobile heading proportions, including 390px and 1024px Echo Chamber layouts. Temporary viewport overrides were reset. No page-content mutation was used to create a favorable result.
3. Read public reference sites and primary usability/design/performance research. Inspected Raycast, Family, Chronicle, Linear, and Nothing visually in a temporary browser tab. Family/Chronicle showed light current designs; Nothing's live product presentation was inspected beyond the sparse search extract. Cookie choices were dismissed without agreeing to optional tracking. The temporary tab was closed and the existing local preview retained.
4. Audited all ten app source trees through three parallel bounded research assignments. Each ledger records exact files, lines, feature gates, configured defaults, and evidence limits. The audit preferred executable paths over README claims or comments.
5. Checked Echo Chamber's public US App Store listing separately from current local source. Listing text is developer-authored public evidence, not an independent runtime certification; its version/offer conflicts are preserved.
6. Submitted the synthesis to independent product-truth reviewers before delivery. Their accepted corrections, if any, are incorporated in the proposal.

## Evidence classes

- **Controlled experiment:** the Vodafone performance case provides an intervention and business outcome in its own setting. It is not a prediction for this website.
- **Usability research:** the NN/G writing study reports task/usability outcomes; heading research supports scanning. Do not relabel these as conversion lifts.
- **Aesthetic experiment:** Tuch et al. reports short-exposure screenshot ratings, not sales.
- **Observational conversion data:** Unbounce's report has selection criteria, heterogeneous conversions, and confounding. It is directional context, not a causal estimate or an appropriate universal target.
- **Design observation:** public reference pages reveal composition, hierarchy, copy, actions, and visible behavior. Their private analytics and conversion rates were not accessible.
- **Static implementation evidence:** a reachable app path supports a qualified feature description. It does not establish correct hardware behavior, shipping availability, localization, or production service provisioning.
- **Public commercial evidence:** Echo Chamber's listing establishes what is currently advertised on the checked storefront, subject to its own internal inconsistencies. Other apps retain development status because a public release was not established.
- **Recommendation:** proposed headlines, narrative order, artwork, interactions, and measurement strategy are design judgments informed by the evidence, not tested winners.
