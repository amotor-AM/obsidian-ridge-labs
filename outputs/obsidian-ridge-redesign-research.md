# Research brief: a product-focused Obsidian Ridge Labs site

September 26, 2026. Research and proposal only; implementation awaits the founder's approval.

The [complete recommendation and draft copy](</Volumes/Lextar/Developer/Obsidian Ridge Labs/docs/research-2026-09-26/RECOMMENDATION.md>) defines the proposed visual direction, homepage sequence, individual arguments for all ten apps, factual corrections, doctrine amendments, and implementation/validation scope.

The recommendation is to keep the studio's conviction and dark visual identity while making the individual products concrete. Give Echo Chamber, Mettle, and Memora equally deliberate scenes. Show the actual result of using each app. Give every product page its own narrative and remove the repeated closing slogan.

## Findings

**Readable evidence is a stronger foundation than promotional intensity.** Descriptive headings support efficient scanning; concise, factual writing has a usability evidence base. That does not establish a guaranteed conversion increase. These findings support an editorial pass in which every section supplies a distinct reason to act and the headings make the argument understandable on their own. [NN/G heading research](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/layer-cake-pattern-scanning/), [original writing study](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/).

**The five reference sites offer design observations, not a conversion leaderboard.** Their public pages connect category, demonstration, task, and action in useful ways. Live inspection confirmed that they have evolved since the styles described in the initial brief. The proposal translates selected principles rather than reconstructing an old appearance or combining unrelated effects. [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/), [Family](https://family.co/), [Chronicle](https://chroniclehq.com/), [Linear](https://linear.app/), [Nothing](https://nothing.tech/products/ear-3a).

**The source contains better sales arguments than the existing summaries.** Mettle preserves the actual progression rule and training evidence behind its prescription. Memora turns a bounded portion of supplied material into draft cards, then supports scheduled recall. Kith supports a complete reach-out loop. Trove separates a free photo-inclusive archive from paid reports. These are concrete reasons to use the products; the audit ledgers below establish the qualifications.

**Some current claims are inaccurate or version-dependent.** Examples include Molehill's claimed absence of streaks, Memora's implied draft editor, Wove's garment-specific packing, and the treatment of Mettle/Cove iCloud as a verified opt-in. Echo Chamber's public listing and newer source have materially different offers. Exact quotas and model options need release mapping before publication. [Echo Chamber listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/echo-chamber-ai-transcription/id6761675060).

**Attractive motion must preserve access and speed.** Performance has direct experimental evidence in a different commercial setting; it is a reason to measure loading and responsiveness here, not to promise the same lift. [Vodafone experiment](https://web.dev/case-studies/vodafone).

Source implementation, public listing evidence, and runtime/release verification are recorded separately. No product binary was executed, no store purchase was made, and no app behavior is represented as hardware-tested during this research.

## Decision

Approve the local website rewrite and design direction in the complete proposal, including the named doctrine changes. Then implement and review the coherent result locally. Resolve remaining release facts before publishing their associated claims. The proposal does not authorize changes to the apps, deployment, App Store metadata, new tracking, or new commercial terms.
